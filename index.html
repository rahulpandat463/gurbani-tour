Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Got doubts regarding Bali visa, currency, food, or weather? Find answers right here:

1. What are the Visa requirements for Indian citizens traveling to Bali?
Indian passport holders can obtain an e-VOA (Electronic Visa on Arrival) online prior to departure or on arrival at Denpasar Airport. The visa fee is 500,000 IDR (approx ₹2,800 / $35 USD) and is valid for 30 days. Your passport must have at least 6 months validity from the date of travel. Our visa concierge team assists you with filling the e-VOA and mandatory Indonesia Customs QR declaration for free.
2. Will pure vegetarian and Jain food be easily available in Bali?
Yes, 100%! All 3 dinners included in this package are hosted at reputed Indian restaurants like Queen's Tandoor, Sitara, and Atithi, offering separate pure vegetarian and Jain cooking kitchens (without onion/garlic on request). Furthermore, our private chauffeur knows the best veg-friendly cafes near Ubud and Uluwatu.
3. Can I customize this 4D/3N itinerary (add Nusa Penida or stay in a Villa)?
Yes! This package is 100% customizable. You can split your stay (e.g. 2 nights in Kuta + 1 night in an Ubud Private Pool Villa) or extend to 5 Nights / 6 Days to include a full-day Nusa Penida Island speedboat tour. Just click 'Get Free Quote' and mention your preferences.
4. Which currency should I carry to Bali from India?
The currency of Bali is the Indonesian Rupiah (IDR). We recommend carrying fresh US Dollars (USD in clean, crisp $50 or $100 notes printed after 2013) or an international zero-forex debit/credit card (like Niyo, Fi, or Scapia). Our chauffeur will also assist you at authorized government money changers in Kuta with zero commission.
5. How does the 20% advance booking policy work?
You only need to pay a 20% initial token amount to confirm your hotels, private chauffeur, and lock in the discounted promotional pricing. The remaining balance can be cleared 10 days before your travel date. We also offer easy 3, 6, and 9-month no-cost EMI options.
