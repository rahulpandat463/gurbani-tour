Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about Gurbani Tours? Here is everything you need to know.

Is Gurbani Tours completely free for travelers?

Yes, 100% free! You never pay any fee to Gurbani Tours for requesting quotes, comparing itineraries, or connecting with travel agents. You only pay for your actual trip package directly to your selected verified agent.

How do I know the travel agents are trustworthy?

Every single travel agent on Gurbani Tours undergoes mandatory GST and business license verification, background checks, and past customer feedback reviews. We only match you with established destination specialists holding 4.5+ star ratings.

Can I customize hotels, flight timings, and cabs?

Absolutely! Unlike rigid booking websites, Gurbani Tours allows 100% customization. You can upgrade to a 5-star hotel, request a private Innova Crysta, add candlelight dinners, or tweak your daily sightseeing schedule.

What if I face any issue during my trip?

Gurbani Tours assigns a dedicated WhatsApp Trip Concierge before departure. If your cab is late or hotel check-in has a delay, our support desk intervenes directly with the local operator in real-time to resolve the matter.
